How it works

  1. Text is cleaned and turned into TF-IDF features.
  2. Image goes through a pretrained MobileNetV3-small; the 576-number vector before the classifier is kept.
  3. Fusion combines both: either one classifier on the joined vectors (early fusion) or a weighted average of two per-modality models (late fusion). Whichever scored best on the validation set is used here.

Everything runs on CPU. Trained on MVSA-Single (Twitter image-text pairs, Niu et al. 2016).
